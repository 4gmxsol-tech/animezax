Winter 2027 anime release dates: confirmed January premieres and THE ONE PIECE

Planning your next watchlist? These are the upcoming anime dates we can verify from official announcements, with unconfirmed details kept separate.

By AnimeZax Editorial·November 11, 2026·5 min read

Winter anime season begins in January, and two major releases already have useful official timing information: Shangri-La Frontier Season 3 starts on January 10, 2027 in Japan, while Netflix’s new THE ONE PIECE adaptation is scheduled for February 2027.

Confirmed upcoming anime release dates

Shangri-La Frontier Season 3 — January 10, 2027

The official Japanese broadcast page lists the third season beginning January 10, 2027, on the MBS/TBS network at 5:00 p.m. on Sundays, with a two-cour run planned. Broadcast and streaming availability outside Japan may differ; check local licensed services closer to launch. Official broadcast schedule.

THE ONE PIECE — February 2027

Netflix’s new adaptation of the East Blue saga is scheduled to premiere in February 2027. Season 1 is planned as seven episodes released together, covering approximately the first 50 manga chapters and leading to Luffy meeting Sanji. Animation is by WIT STUDIO. Netflix Media Center: THE ONE PIECE.

What about the rest of the Winter 2027 anime lineup?

More January–March titles are being announced, and some listings still show only a month or season rather than an exact day. We will add a precise date only when it is supported by an official broadcaster, distributor or production announcement. A seasonal listing is not the same as a confirmed worldwide streaming date.

How to check an anime release date in your country

  1. Start with the anime’s official website or the production committee’s announcement.
  2. Check the licensed streaming service available in your region.
  3. Confirm the time zone: a Japanese broadcast date can fall on the previous calendar day in another country.
  4. Recheck the schedule during premiere week in case of a programming change.
