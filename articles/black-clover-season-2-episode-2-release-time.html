Black Clover Season 2 Episode 2: release date and time

The Magic Knights’ battle in the Spade Kingdom continues. Here is the confirmed Japanese broadcast time and the currently announced Japan streaming schedule.

AnimeZax Editorial·October 9, 2026·3 min read

Black Clover Season 2 Episode 2 is scheduled to air in Japan on Saturday, October 10, 2026, at 11:00 p.m. JST. That is 4:00 p.m. in Sudan (UTC+2). The episode’s Japanese title is 「地獄への扉」, commonly rendered as “The Door to Hell.”

When does Black Clover Season 2 Episode 2 come out?

The official Japanese announcement lists the episode for October 10 at 23:00 on TV Tokyo and affiliated stations. Broadcast schedules are Japan-specific, so local TV and streaming times may differ.

Sudan time: 4:00 p.m. on Saturday, October 10, if the published 23:00 JST broadcast time is followed. This is a time-zone conversion, not confirmation that a local broadcaster or streaming service carries the episode at that hour.

What happens in Episode 2?

The official preview describes the Magic Knights’ fight against the Dark Triad in the Spade Kingdom. Mereoleona faces the demon that appears through Morris’s actions, while Charlotte, Yuno, Jack, and Nacht confront dangerous opponents.
