Crunchyroll Anime Awards vs Nippon Anime Awards: what is the difference?

Two major anime award shows now run in Japan. One is run by a streaming service and voted on partly by fans. The other is brand new and run by the industry. Here is how they compare.

By AnimeZax Editorial·November 28, 2026·4 min read

If you follow anime news, you will see "anime awards" headlines often. They can mean very different events. This guide separates the two biggest ones, using dates and details published by Crunchyroll and the Association of Japanese Animations.

Crunchyroll Anime Awards

Crunchyroll's awards have run since January 2017. Since 2023 the ceremony has been held at the Grand Prince Hotel New Takanawa in Tokyo. The most recent one, the 10th ceremony, took place on May 23, 2026, and the final season of My Hero Academia won Anime of the Year.

How Crunchyroll picks winners

There are two rounds. Judges first submit up to five candidates per category, and the six with the most nominations become finalists. Then fans vote publicly for one week. Judges count for 70 percent and the public for 30 percent, a ratio used since at least the 6th edition. Eligibility covers anime released from January to December of the previous year. Crunchyroll usually announces the categories around December.

Nippon Anime Awards

The Association of Japanese Animations (AJA) announced this new show on November 2 at the end of the Tokyo Anime Next event. The first ceremony is set for November 21, 2027 at SGC HALL ARIAKE in Ariake, Tokyo, organised with the Tokyo Metropolitan Government. Categories have not been announced yet.

What it replaces

The AJA ran the Tokyo Anime Award Festival from 2014 to 2026. Its last edition was held in March, where Mobile Suit Gundam GQuuuuuuX won the TV anime award and Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba Infinity Castle I won the film award.

Quick comparison

  • Organiser: Crunchyroll (streaming service) vs the AJA with the Tokyo Metropolitan Government.
  • Next ceremony: to be announced, with categories around December, vs November 21, 2027.
  • Venue: Tokyo (Grand Prince Hotel New Takanawa in 2026) vs SGC HALL ARIAKE, Tokyo.
  • Fan vote: yes, 30 percent weight vs not announced.
