Demon Slayer Watch Order: The Best Way to Watch Every Arc

Want to start Demon Slayer without getting caught between seasons, movies and recap releases? This guide gives you a simple viewing order, explains the Mugen Train choice and keeps optional material separate from the main story.

AnimeZax Editorial·Updated November 18, 2026·7 min read

The easiest way to watch Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba is to follow the main story arcs in their original release order. The only choice that regularly confuses new viewers is Mugen Train: it was released as a film and later adapted into a television arc. You only need one version for the main story.

Quick answer: Watch Season 1 → Mugen Train (movie or TV arc) → Entertainment District → Swordsmith Village → Hashira Training. Treat compilation films and recap specials as optional unless you specifically want a cinema-style refresher.

1. Start with Season 1

Begin with the first television season, which introduces Tanjiro Kamado, Nezuko and the central conflict. It establishes the world, the rules of demon slaying and the relationships that make later arcs meaningful. Do not start with a movie or a later season: the character introductions and emotional stakes build from the beginning.

2. Watch the Mugen Train story once

After Season 1, move to the Mugen Train arc. You have two main options:

  • The Mugen Train movie: the original feature-film presentation. Choose this if you prefer one continuous cinematic experience.
  • The Mugen Train TV arc: an episodic adaptation that includes an additional first episode and some expanded scenes. Choose this if you prefer television episodes and extra context.

The TV adaptation retells the same central journey. Watching the full movie and then every episode of the TV version is largely repetitive. If you choose the TV arc, follow it as the next story section after Season 1.

3. Continue with the Entertainment District Arc

Next, watch the Entertainment District Arc. It follows directly from the Mugen Train events and develops the main cast through a new mission. Keep the release order rather than jumping to later character-focused clips or compilation edits.

4. Watch the Swordsmith Village Arc

Continue with the Swordsmith Village Arc. This section expands the wider Demon Slayer Corps and introduces important characters and abilities. It assumes you already know the earlier cast, so it works best after the Entertainment District.

5. Follow with the Hashira Training Arc

The Hashira Training Arc comes after Swordsmith Village in the television viewing sequence. It prepares the story for the next major conflict and is not a replacement for any of the earlier arcs. Watch it after the preceding seasons to avoid missing character context.

Do I need to watch the compilation movies?

Not usually. Some theatrical releases combine or preview material from television arcs. They can be useful as a recap or a big-screen event, but they do not replace the complete story sequence for a first-time viewer. Check the film's official synopsis before watching so you know whether it contains new episodes, recap footage or preview material.

Can I skip filler in Demon Slayer?

Demon Slayer is relatively straightforward compared with long-running anime that have extensive anime-original filler lists. Rather than relying on an unsourced skip list, follow the main episodes in release order. If a platform labels a recap or compilation as a separate special, treat it as optional unless you want a refresher.

How to avoid spoilers while watching

  1. Use the arc order above and avoid searching character names before their introductions.
  2. Choose episode guides that clearly label spoiler sections.
  3. Be careful with thumbnails and recommendation feeds, which may reveal later character designs or outcomes.
  4. Save discussion threads until you finish the arc you are watching.

Frequently asked questions

Should I watch the Mugen Train movie or the TV version?

Either works. Choose the movie for a single cinematic experience or the TV arc for an episodic version with an additional opening episode and expanded material. You do not need both in full.

Can I skip the Mugen Train arc?

No, not if you want to follow the main story. It is an important bridge between Season 1 and the Entertainment District Arc.

Where do I go after Hashira Training?

Continue with the next officially released story instalment in the franchise. Check the official anime channels and licensed distributors for current release and regional availability rather than relying on unverified dates.
