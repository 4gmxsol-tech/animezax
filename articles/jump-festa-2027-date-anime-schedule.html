Jump Festa 2027 date and anime schedule: Jujutsu Kaisen, One Piece, Naruto and more

Jump Festa 2027 takes place on December 19–20, 2026. See the confirmed Jump Super Stage and Jump Studio timetable, the announced Jump Station Online lineup, and Japan-to-Sudan time conversions.

By AnimeZax Editorial·November 11, 2026·5 min read

Fans waiting for anime announcements have a major date to mark: Jump Festa 2027 is scheduled for Saturday, December 19 and Sunday, December 20, 2026, at Makuhari Messe in Chiba, Japan. The official programme lists stage appearances for Jujutsu Kaisen, Naruto, One Piece, Chainsaw Man, Demon Slayer and Black Clover.

When is Jump Festa 2027?

The event runs December 19–20, 2026, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Japan Standard Time (JST), with final admission at 4:30 p.m. and some stage programmes continuing later. The venue is Makuhari Messe International Exhibition Halls 1–9 and 11. Admission is free but requires advance application and an invitation ticket through Jump NAVI. The regular admission application window is November 2–16, 2026, closing at 5:59 p.m. JST. The organizer says all attendees aged elementary-school level and above need an electronic admission ticket. See the official admission application guide for eligibility and current status.
