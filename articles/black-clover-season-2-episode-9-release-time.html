Black Clover Season 2 Episode 9: release date, time and where to watch

Episode 9, "Yuno Grinberryall", arrives on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Here are the times by region and where to stream it.

By AnimeZax Editorial·November 28, 2026·3 min read

Black Clover returned on October 3, 2026 after a long hiatus and releases weekly on Saturdays. Episode 9 is the 179th episode overall.

Release date and title

Episode 9 is titled "Yuno Grinberryall" and is scheduled for Saturday, November 28, 2026. It airs on TV Tokyo at 11:00 p.m. JST, and international streaming follows an hour later.

Release times by region

  • 8:00 a.m. Pacific Time
  • 10:00 a.m. Central Time
  • 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time
  • 4:00 p.m. UK time and Central European Time (as listed by CBR)
  • 8:30 p.m. India Standard Time
  • 6:00 p.m. in Sudan (Central Africa Time, UTC+2), converted from the 4:00 p.m. GMT time

Where to watch

The Japanese audio and English subtitle version streams on Crunchyroll. The English dub follows a separate schedule and is reported to be two episodes behind, which points to December 12 for episode 9. In Japan, Anime Times, Lemino, U-NEXT and Anime Hodai start early streaming at 12:00 p.m. JST on November 29.

About the early leak

Screen Rant reports that Crunchyroll briefly published episode 9 alongside episode 8 on November 21 and then removed it. If you want to avoid spoilers, mute related tags until the official release.
