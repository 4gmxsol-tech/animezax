Can visitors download videos?

We only enable a download link for a direct video file that AnimeZax owns or has permission to distribute, and only when the license permits downloads. For third-party services, use their official offline-download feature where available. A browser button cannot lawfully or reliably extract a video from an embedded streaming service.

For a future authorized file, the HTML5 player can expose native playback controls; a separate download link should be enabled only after confirming the file's license and direct URL.
