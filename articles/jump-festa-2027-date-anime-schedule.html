Jump Festa 2027 date and anime schedule: Jujutsu Kaisen, One Piece, Naruto and more

Jump Festa 2027 takes place on December 19–20, 2026. Here are the announced anime stage slots, Japan time conversions and the official pages to follow.

By AnimeZax Editorial·November 11, 2026·5 min read

Fans waiting for anime announcements have a major date to mark: Jump Festa 2027 is scheduled for Saturday, December 19 and Sunday, December 20, 2026, at Makuhari Messe in Chiba, Japan. The official programme lists stage appearances for Jujutsu Kaisen, Naruto, One Piece, Chainsaw Man, Demon Slayer and Black Clover.

When is Jump Festa 2027?

The event runs December 19–20, 2026, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Japan Standard Time (JST), with some stage programmes continuing later. The venue is Makuhari Messe International Exhibition Halls 1–9 and 11. Admission is free but requires advance application and a ticket through the official Jump NAVI process. Check the official guide for current application status and conditions.

What about Black Clover?

Black Clover is listed for the Jump Station Online programme on Sunday, December 20 at 1:00 p.m. JST, or 6:00 a.m. in Sudan. This is a separate online stage listing, not the main Jump Super Stage.

Will new anime seasons or release dates be announced?

Jump Festa is a major franchise event, so announcements are possible, but a scheduled stage appearance does not guarantee a new season, trailer or release date. AnimeZax will label confirmed announcements separately from predictions and fan speculation.

How to follow Jump Festa 2027

  1. Check the official Jump Festa 2027 website.
  2. Verify series and times on the official Super Stage programme.
  3. Check the Jump Station Online schedule for Black Clover.
  4. Recheck the programme close to the event because schedules can change.

Frequently asked questions

What dates is Jump Festa 2027?

December 19 and 20, 2026, in Japan. The event is called Jump Festa 2027 even though it takes place in December 2026.

What time is the Jujutsu Kaisen stage?

The official schedule lists it on December 19 from 3:25 to 3:55 p.m. JST, or 8:25 to 8:55 a.m. in Sudan.

When is the One Piece stage?

The official programme lists ONE PIECE on December 20 from 4:45 to 5:35 p.m. JST, or 9:45 to 10:35 a.m. in Sudan.
