Dragon Ball: the practical watch order

Dragon Ball Watch Order

Dragon Ball has several series and movie releases. For a first-time viewer, the cleanest approach is to follow the major television storyline in release order rather than mixing every movie into it.

Quick answer: For a first watch, prioritize the main television story in release order. Optional recap material can wait until you want a refresher.

1. Dragon Ball

Begin with the original Dragon Ball series to experience Goku's early journey and the foundations of the franchise.

2. Dragon Ball Z or Kai

Continue with Dragon Ball Z for the original broadcast experience, or Dragon Ball Kai for a more streamlined version of the same broad story.

3. Dragon Ball Super

Move to Dragon Ball Super after Z/Kai. Movies and specials can be explored separately once you know the core timeline.

Release schedules, episode listings, regional availability, and franchise announcements can change. Verify current details with official distributors and local sources before planning a watch.
