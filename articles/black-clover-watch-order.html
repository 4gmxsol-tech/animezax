Black Clover watch order: episodes and movie

Starting Black Clover or catching up for Season 2? Use this spoiler-aware viewing order, then check the latest premiere and regional streaming details.

By AnimeZax Editorial·November 1, 2026·7 min read

Black Clover is a fantasy-action series about Asta, a boy born without magic in a world where magical ability shapes everyday life. His rivalry with Yuno and his ambition to become Wizard King drive a story full of squads, battles, and escalating challenges.

What is Black Clover about? (spoiler-free)

In the Clover Kingdom, magic is everything — it shapes jobs, status, and power. Asta was born without any magic at all, while his childhood rival Yuno is a magical prodigy. Both boys dream of the same thing: becoming the Wizard King, the kingdom's strongest mage. Asta refuses to give up, and instead of magic he fights with anti-magic — the power to cancel other people's spells — and sheer determination.

Who will enjoy it: if you like classic shonen energy — training, tournaments, rivalries, and a hero who wins through persistence rather than talent — Black Clover is a strong pick. The first episodes are fast and loud; the story settles into longer arcs once the squads and the wider world are established. With 170 episodes in the original run, it is a substantial watch, but far shorter than One Piece or Naruto.

1. Start with the TV series

For a first watch, begin with episode one and follow the TV series in release order. The early episodes establish the Clover Kingdom, the Magic Knights, Asta and Yuno’s relationship, and the different squads. Avoid jumping into later arcs before you know the characters and their motivations.

2. Where does the movie fit?

Black Clover: Sword of the Wizard King is a feature film. If you are new to the franchise, the simplest approach is to watch the main series first — all 170 episodes — then watch the film once you are comfortable with the setting and cast. The movie is a standalone story, so it works best after episode 170 rather than in the middle of the series. Check the film’s official listing for its placement and availability in your region.

3. A spoiler-safe catch-up method

  • Review the names of the Magic Knight squads and their captains.
  • Remember that Asta’s determination and anti-magic are central to how he approaches fights.
  • Keep track of the main rivalries and alliances, but avoid reading arc summaries beyond the episodes you have watched.
  • When searching for a character, add “spoiler-free” to your query and avoid image results if you are especially sensitive to spoilers.

Crunchyroll streams the Black Clover TV series in many regions, with subtitled and dubbed options, and also carries Season 2. The movie Black Clover: Sword of the Wizard King (2023) streams on Netflix. Availability changes by country and over time, so check your local catalogue before subscribing.
