Story overview

Yuji Itadori has extraordinary physical abilities and a strong instinct to help others. After a dangerous encounter involving a cursed object, he becomes entangled with jujutsu sorcerers who fight threats born from human negativity.

Why watch Jujutsu Kaisen?

The series combines fast-paced supernatural action with a structured power system, ensemble dynamics, and questions about responsibility and the value of a life. It contains graphic violence and darker themes.

Key characters

Yuji Itadori

A compassionate student forced to make difficult choices in a dangerous supernatural world.

Megumi Fushiguro

A reserved sorcerer whose technique gives him a tactical approach to combat.

Satoru Gojo

A powerful and unconventional teacher at Tokyo Jujutsu High.
