Story overview

In a world where superpowers are common, Izuku Midoriya dreams of becoming a hero despite being born without a Quirk. The series combines its central premise with character-driven stakes and a distinctive world that rewards viewers who follow the story arc by arc.

Why watch My Hero Academia?

It combines superhero spectacle with training arcs, rivalries, friendships, and a large ensemble cast.
