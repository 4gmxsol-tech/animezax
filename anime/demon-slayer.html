Story overview

After a demon attack changes his family forever, Tanjiro Kamado sets out to find a way to restore his sister Nezuko and prevent other families from suffering the same fate. His journey brings him into the Demon Slayer Corps and a world of dangerous supernatural enemies.

Why watch Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba?

The series pairs a clear emotional motivation with training, teamwork, distinctive sword techniques, and visually expressive battles. Its themes of compassion and perseverance sit alongside intense violence and frightening imagery.

Key characters

Tanjiro Kamado

A determined and empathetic swordsman guided by his care for others.

Nezuko Kamado

Tanjiro’s sister, whose transformation is central to the story.

Zenitsu Agatsuma

A fearful but capable fighter whose reactions often provide comic contrast.
