Spy x Family

A spy, an assassin, and a telepath form a pretend family while hiding their secrets from one another.

Story overview

To complete a high-stakes mission, spy Twilight must create a family and enroll his child in an elite school. His adopted daughter can read minds, and his new wife has a secret life of her own. Each family member hides the truth while gradually forming genuine bonds.

Why watch Spy x Family?

Spy x Family balances secret-agent missions with domestic comedy and sincere found-family moments. Its episodic humor makes it approachable, while the characters’ hidden identities create an ongoing source of tension.

Key characters

Loid Forger

A meticulous spy who must perform the role of a devoted father.

Anya Forger

A telepathic child whose thoughts—and misunderstandings—drive much of the comedy.

Yor Forger

A kind office worker with a secret career as an assassin.
