Black Clover watch order: episodes and movie

Starting Black Clover or catching up for Season 2? Use this spoiler-aware viewing order, then check the latest premiere and regional streaming details.

By AnimeZax Editorial·October 2, 2026·5 min read

Black Clover is a fantasy-action series about Asta, a boy born without magic in a world where magical ability shapes everyday life. His rivalry with Yuno and his ambition to become Wizard King drive a story full of squads, battles, and escalating challenges.

Black Clover Season 2: latest release update

The Japanese TV broadcast is scheduled to begin on October 3, 2026, at 11:00 p.m. JST. Streaming schedules may differ by platform and country. Read our detailed Season 2 release date, time, and where to watch guide before checking your local streaming catalog.

1. Start with the TV series

For a first watch, begin with episode one and follow the TV series in release order. The early episodes establish the Clover Kingdom, the Magic Knights, Asta and Yuno’s relationship, and the different squads. Avoid jumping into later arcs before you know the characters and their motivations.

2. Where does the movie fit?

Black Clover: Sword of the Wizard King is a feature film. If you are new to the franchise, the simplest approach is to watch the main series first, then watch the film once you are comfortable with the setting and cast. Check the film’s official listing for its placement and availability in your region.
