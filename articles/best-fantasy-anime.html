GENRE GUIDE

Where to start if you love magic-based anime

Fantasy anime can mean tactical magic battles, quiet journeys, sprawling adventures, or stories about the cost of power. Start with the kind of fantasy you want.

By AnimeZax Editorial·October 2, 2026·5 min read

“Fantasy anime” covers very different experiences. The most useful question is not which series is universally best, but which kind of world and storytelling you want to spend time in.

For magic battles and rivalries: Black Clover

Choose Black Clover if you enjoy a determined underdog, squad dynamics, escalating battles, and a clear long-term ambition. It is a fit for viewers who like momentum and character growth over a long run.

For a reflective journey: Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End

Frieren approaches fantasy through memory, time, grief, and the relationships that remain after a grand adventure. Its quieter rhythm makes it a different choice from a battle-focused series.

For a huge adventure: One Piece

One Piece offers a broad world, a large cast, and a journey built around dreams, friendship, and discovery. The episode count is substantial, so consider whether you want a long-running commitment.

For fast-paced power progression: Solo Leveling

Solo Leveling centers on hunters, dangerous dungeons, and a protagonist who grows stronger through escalating challenges. It suits viewers looking for a more direct action hook.

Pick the one that fits your mood

Choose battles and rivalries for energy, reflective fantasy for character-focused storytelling, a sprawling adventure for a long journey, or power progression for a focused action premise. Try one episode and decide whether the tone works for you.
