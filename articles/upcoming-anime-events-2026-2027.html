Upcoming anime events: dates, places and what to expect

The next months bring some of the biggest stage events in anime. Here are the dates, venues and what each event is for.

By AnimeZax Editorial·November 28, 2026·4 min read

Anime news tends to break at live events: new seasons, trailers, cast stages and award shows. This guide lists the main events coming up, with dates as published by the organisers or press at the time of writing.

Jump Festa 2027: December 19-20, 2026

Shueisha's yearly Weekly Shonen Jump festival takes place at Makuhari Messe in Chiba, Japan. The Jump Super Stage lists casts for One Piece (December 20, 16:45-17:35 JST), Jujutsu Kaisen, Demon Slayer, Chainsaw Man, Dan Da Dan, Spy x Family, Haikyu!!, Naruto and more. Entry is free but needs advance application, and the organisers require an address in Japan, so most fans will follow along through news coverage. Big announcements often come out of these stages.

Comiket 109: December 29-31, 2026

Comic Market is the largest market for self-published manga and doujin works, held at Tokyo Big Sight with cosplay areas and company booths. This winter edition runs for three days.

AnimeJapan 2027: March 27-28, 2027

The industry's spring expo moves to INTEX Osaka for the first time, so it is not in Tokyo this year. Expect studio booths, voice actor stages and trailers for upcoming seasons. Public days are March 27 and 28.

Anime Expo 2027: July 2-5, 2027

North America's main anime convention returns to the Los Angeles Convention Center. This date comes from a convention tracker, so check the official site before booking travel.

Nippon Anime Awards: November 21, 2027

The Association of Japanese Animations announced a new award show, replacing the Tokyo Anime Award Festival. The first ceremony is planned for SGC HALL ARIAKE in Tokyo. Categories have not been announced yet.

Before you plan a trip

Dates, ticket rules and lineups can change. Check each organiser's official site before you book anything. We will update this guide as details change.
